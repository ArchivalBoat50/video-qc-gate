## PART 1 — DEFECTS (hard gate; any of these fails regardless of craft)
1. IDENTITY DRIFT — face shape, jaw, nose, or apparent age changing across
   frames. Most damaging possible defect; the consistent face IS the asset.
2. ANATOMY BREAKS — finger count or merging changing between frames, limbs
   bending wrong, a hand dissolving into blur, impossible foot positions.
3. WARDROBE CONTINUITY — clothing or footwear changing colour, type or shape.
4. OBJECT INSTABILITY — held items changing shape, size or grip. Straps,
   jewelry, hat logos morphing. Objects appearing or vanishing.
5. BACKGROUND MELT — scenery sliding unnaturally, warped horizons.
6. FAKE UI — any phone status bar, app chrome, or invented interface rendered
   into the frame. Automatic fail.
7. GARBLED TEXT — fake lettering on clothing or signage. Only fail if large
   and central; note it otherwise. Do NOT confuse this with the intentional
   burned-in caption, which is real text and is supposed to be there.

## PART 2 — CAPTION LEGIBILITY (this stage only)
The post carries a short HOOK pinned near the top and a BODY block in the
middle third, white text with a dark border.
a. Is every line of both blocks readable at a glance against the footage behind
   it? White text over bright sky or a pale surface with a thin border is the
   common failure.
b. Does any line run off the edge of the frame or collide with the other block?
c. Does the caption sit over the subject's face or over the visual point of
   interest in a way that hurts the shot?
Illegible or clipped captions are a "fail" — the caption is the whole post.

## PART 3 — CRAFT. Answer each yes/no:
a. HOOK — would a brand-new viewer stop scrolling within the first second?
   Frame 1 is the hook. A static, unremarkable opening frame is a no.
b. 10-SECOND HOLD — is there enough visual or textual interest to hold a
   viewer 10+ seconds? Short clips with sparse captions usually fail this.
c. CRAFT — visually appealing and intentional, reading as a real amateur photo
   rather than AI-glam or a catalog pose.
